YouTube Shorts Editing Mistakes That Kill Reach

Reach drops when videos are hard to follow, too slow, or visually inconsistent.

Mistake 1: Slow intro

If the first second has no payoff, users swipe. Open with a visual or verbal hook.

Mistake 2: Tiny captions

Unreadable text kills retention. Keep captions large and high-contrast.

Mistake 3: No story flow

Even short clips need setup, action, and result. Random cuts reduce completion.

Mistake 4: Weak ending

Close with a clear CTA, loop point, or teaser to push rewatch behavior.
