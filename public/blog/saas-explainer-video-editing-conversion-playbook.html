SaaS Explainer Video Editing Conversion Playbook

SaaS videos fail when they are feature-heavy and outcome-light. Editing should simplify and persuade.

Lead with the pain point

Show the audience problem before product features. Relevance increases watch depth.

Show outcomes, then workflow

Users care about speed, savings, and results. Show outcomes early and then explain how.

Use clean on-screen hierarchy

Zoom on key UI moments and remove clutter with focused framing.

Cut to trust signals

Add testimonials, metric overlays, and customer logos to reduce friction.
